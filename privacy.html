Faclair: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Faclair is a Scottish Gaelic–English dictionary and grammar guide for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

What we collect

Nothing. Faclair does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your favourites and settings (for example, whether definitions are shown in English or Gàidhlig) are stored only on your device, using the system's standard app settings storage. They are never sent anywhere.

Links to other websites

The dictionary works offline. If you tap Listen on LearnGaelic or a source link, the app opens that website (LearnGaelic, Ainmean-Àite na h-Alba, Ordnance Survey or Wikimedia Commons). Those sites have their own privacy policies; Faclair sends them nothing except the request for the page you chose, such as the word you looked up.

Children

Faclair is suitable for learners of all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
