Faclair: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Faclair is a Scottish Gaelic–English dictionary and grammar guide for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

What we collect

Nothing, unless you choose to send a suggestion (see below). Faclair has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares information about you.

What stays on your device

Your favourites and settings (for example, whether definitions are shown in English or Gàidhlig) are stored only on your device, using the system's standard app settings storage. They are never sent anywhere.

Suggesting a correction

Each word, place and grammar chapter has a Suggest a correction button. If you use it and press Send, Faclair saves your suggestion, the entry it is about, the app version and, if you typed one, your name, privately in Faclair's own iCloud storage (Apple CloudKit) for the developer to read. Suggestions are never shown in the app or to other users. Apple does not give the developer your Apple ID or email address. If iCloud isn't available on your device, Faclair instead opens your own email app with the same message; nothing is sent unless you press Send there, and the developer then receives your email address in the usual way.

Suggestions are used only to improve the dictionary. If a suggestion is used, your name (if you gave one) may be credited in the dictionary's list of changes; email addresses are never published or shared. To have a suggestion deleted, contact the developer through the support page. Your name, if you enter it, is remembered on your device for next time.

Maps

When you choose Show on map on a place-name page, Faclair asks Apple Maps where that place is, sending the place name. This uses Apple's map service under Apple's privacy policy; Faclair does not send your location.

Links to other websites

The dictionary works offline. If you tap Listen on LearnGaelic or a source link, the app opens that website (for example LearnGaelic, Ainmean-Àite na h-Alba, Ordnance Survey, Wikimedia Commons or the courses and dictionaries in Learning more). Those sites have their own privacy policies; Faclair sends them nothing except the request for the page you chose, such as the word you looked up.

Children

Faclair is suitable for learners of all ages. It asks for no personal information; a name in a suggestion is optional.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: see the support page.
