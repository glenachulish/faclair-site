Faclair: Support

Faclair is a free Scottish Gaelic–English dictionary with a grammar guide, for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Contact

For help, or to report a mistake in an entry, email 88_intake.troupes@icloud.com. Please include the word and what you think it should say. Corrections from Gaelic speakers are very welcome.

Common questions

How do I see Gaelic definitions? Open any entry that has one and choose Gàidhlig or Both at the top.

How do I hear a word? Tap Listen on LearnGaelic on any entry. Some words and place names also have a recording in the app.

Does it need the internet? No. Only the LearnGaelic button and source links need a connection.

Can I search without accents? Yes: aite finds àite, and lenited or plural forms find their headword.
